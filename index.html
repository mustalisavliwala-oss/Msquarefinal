— Who We Are

Quality, precision and customer satisfaction, in every sheet.

We manufacture felt, foam and rubber products using premium raw materials and advanced production techniques, from our workshop in Nagpada. Whether it's a standard size or a custom-cut solution, our team works to deliver exceptional craftsmanship, competitive pricing and timely delivery.

We've supplied felt, foam and rubber sheet materials to workshops, manufacturers and traders across Mumbai for generations — from gaskets and packaging to horse saddling and craft felt.
